The Announcement

A second Neighborhood Hotel in New Buffalo.

115 Barton is the newest addition to the Neighborhood collection, and our second property in New Buffalo, just around the corner from The Neighborhood Hotel New Buffalo. Together, the two give guests even more room to spread out in the beach town everyone already loves. An opening date isn't set in stone yet, but we're targeting Fall 2027.

The Story

Summer at Neighborhood Barton.

The design tells of generational visits to our evolving lakeside town, an evolution of campy patterns, quilted fabrics, and pieces that feel modern yet uniquely vintage, as if picked up along the Red Arrow Highway. Inspired by Midwest retreats, family lake houses, and supper clubs, past and present.

The Details

Built for gathering, made to return to.

The lobby icebox is stocked and ready for your favorite sips. Guest room headboards, woven and striped, echo childhood memories of the lake. Twenty-one studio, one-, and two-bedroom suites complement our ten-room inn next door, giving families and groups room to stay awhile, and come back for years to come.
